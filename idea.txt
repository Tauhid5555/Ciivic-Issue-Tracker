# Local Civic Issue Reporter & Tracker

A platform where people can report local problems like broken streetlights, potholes, garbage, drainage issues, and noise complaints.

Right now, people may report these problems by calling a helpline or posting in community groups. But they often cannot easily track their complaints or know if the problem has been fixed.

The platform will let people report an issue by adding details, a photo, and the location. They will also be able to see the status of their report and get updates.

Authorities will be able to see the reported issues, manage them, update their status, and mark them as resolved.

The main goal is to make reporting local problems easier and give people a simple way to track their complaints.
