# Design Concept

## Working title

**Friendly Civic Accountability**

## One-paragraph statement

The product feels like a **trusted neighbor who happens to work for the city**. Visually warm but not childish; plain-spoken, never bureaucratic; status-forward so people always know what happened. Mobile-first because that's where the report happens, but coherent across breakpoints. Photography and human-scale language carry the warmth; structure and clarity carry the trust. Every screen answers one question: *"What happened with my report?"* — even when nothing has happened yet.

## Three values this implies

1. **Warmth over sterility** — soft surfaces, human copy, real photos. Not government-blue gradients.
2. **Status as hero** — every screen surfaces *where the report is* as the largest, most legible element. The "wait" is the product.
3. **Plain over clever** — "Add a photo" not "Upload evidence." "We'll look at this" not "Ticket triaged."

## Three values it rules out

- No dark / enterprise / SaaS-density aesthetics.
- No playful illustration mascots — civic trust wants adults, not cartoons.
- No gamification, badges, leaderboards — this isn't a civic engagement *game*.

## How it shows up per product surface

| Surface | Translation |
|---------|-------------|
| Citizen Portal | Warm, low-friction, reassuring. The reporter should feel heard within seconds. |
| Authority Dashboard | Same color & type system, denser information density. Staff are power users, not anxious; trust = speed. |
| Emails | Same warmth, plain prose, real sender ("ReportIt"), not "noreply@". |

## Anti-references (what we are NOT)

- Generic SaaS dashboard (Stripe / Linear / Notion aesthetic) for the citizen side.
- Government form-heavy (IRS / DMV) anywhere.
- Engagement-game civic app (Nextdoor drama, Twitter-for-city).

## References that fit (for visual tone)

- **NYC 311** — clean, photo-forward, plain copy.
- **FixMyStreet** — mobile-first, location-pinned, lightweight.
- **GOV.UK design system** — accessibility baseline, plain English.
- **Apple Maps incident reporting** — calm, photography-forward.
