How it works

  • 📷
    1. Snap a photo Take a picture of the problem and tell us roughly where it is.
  • 🆔
    2. Get a tracking ID We'll give you a short code the moment your report is submitted.
  • ✉
    3. Get updates by email We'll let you know when the city looks at it, starts work, and resolves it.
  • 🔁
    4. Tell us if it wasn't fixed If something's still wrong, flag it and a staff member will take another look.

What happens after you submit

From report to resolution — usually in under a week.

1 Submit a report Photo + location 2 Confirmation Tracking ID issued 3 Acknowledged Triage in progress 4 In progress Work has started 5 Resolved Done — verify & close 6 Disputed Flag for re-review
