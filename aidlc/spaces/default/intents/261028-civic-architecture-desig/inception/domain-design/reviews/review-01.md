### Summary

The catalogue is structurally sound (acyclic, symmetric, ownership clean), the Q1 to Q4 answers are reflected, and the ADRs are complete with two or more alternatives and a security note. NOT-READY on three Major gaps that a developer would have to guess at: the photo-attach step against the 24-hour orphan expiry, a missing purge path for emails held in Notifications, and an undecided transactional guarantee for notification events.

Suggestion for the human only: the Resolved-versus-closed retention clock (OQ-A5) remains open, and an exact public pin (OQ-A2) is accepted as a decided privacy trade-off.
